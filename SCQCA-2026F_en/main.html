Course Positioning

The course proceeds from the computation model to the qubit Hamiltonian, then sequentially addresses qubit design, control, coupling, readout, calibration, benchmarking, stabilizer QEC, surface-code cycle, cryogenic systems, electronics, packaging, fabrication processes, and a full-system capstone.
