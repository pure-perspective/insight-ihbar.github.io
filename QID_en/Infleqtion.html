Infleqtion

2025.10.13

Infleqtion uses neutral atoms as its core technology, providing quantum computers, precision quantum sensors, and software solutions. It serves clients across government, enterprise, and research institutions, with deployments in the US, UK, Japan, and Australia. In September 2025, it was announced that the company will go public via a merger transaction with Churchill Capital Corp X.

Technology and R&D

  • Core Roadmap: Neutral-Atom Quantum Cores, emphasizing high scalability and precise control. Officially referred to as "nature's perfect qubits, scalable to millions."

  • Error Correction: Utilizes an advanced error correction platform with "dual-species atomic arrays" and "low-crosstalk measurement." Fast and precise operations enhance long-term stability.

  • Quantum Sensing Focus: Atomic clocks, quantum RF receivers, and inertial measurement, targeting critical infrastructure, national security, and geospatial intelligence (including GPS-denied environments).

Products / Services

  • Quantum Computing (Sqale): Neutral atom quantum computer and software stack, featuring a scalable and fault-tolerant roadmap. Deeply integrated with Superstaq software (Infleqtion's optimization compiler) and provides open-source front-ends for Cirq and Qiskit.

  • Quantum Sensing (Sqywire)

    • Tiqker Atomic Clock (a precision time reference).

    • Quantum RF Receiver.

    • Inertial Sensing (for navigation in GPS-denied environments, etc.).

  • Software and Algorithms: Quantum software and tools like "Contextual Machine Learning" for applications in algorithms, optimization, and simulation. Infleqtion's quantum software, Superstaq, enhances the capabilities of quantum hardware.

  • Quantum Cores (Quantum Core Modules): The foundational technology platform for computing and sensing products, emphasizing "nature's perfect qubits, scalable to millions."
