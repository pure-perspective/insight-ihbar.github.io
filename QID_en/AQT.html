AQT

Alpine Quantum Technologies GmbH

2025.10.09

AQT, located in Innsbruck, Austria, is a supplier and R&D company specializing in trapped-ion quantum computers. It features a dual 19-inch rack quantum system that operates at room temperature and can be placed in a data center. It also provides the ARNICA cloud service and simulators to support research and industrial applications (finance, chemistry, logistics, etc.).

AQT's core products are trapped-ion quantum computers and related cloud, simulation, and control components. The company actively participates in European quantum computing infrastructure and hybrid HPC-quantum computing projects, positioning itself as a core supplier in the quantum computing industry chain.

Products / Services

  • Rack-mounted Trapped-Ion Quantum Computer: Dual 19-inch rack, operates at room temperature with power consumption under 2 kW, requires no special cooling or water supply; modular and scalable; has been used in various quantum computing application cases.

  • Cloud and Toolchain (ARNICA): Provides access to the latest quantum computers and simulators; available with a "universal quantum gate set"; supports connectors for multiple quantum SDKs; provides API documentation; an offline high-fidelity simulator facilitates prototyping and performance estimation.

  • Hardware Component Ecosystem: PINE (modular ion trap architecture), BEECH (laser stabilization for demanding applications), ROWAN (laser pulse generation and global control), to accelerate research and enhance experimental performance.

  • Solution-Oriented: Targeting researchers, industry, and public institutions, and supporting integration with High-Performance Computing (HPC) data centers and cloud accessibility.

  • EuroHPC Quantum Computer Inauguration (PIAST‑Q): Marks a significant milestone in the construction of European quantum computing capabilities, demonstrating public sector adoption and regional strategic support.

  • Hybrid Quantum-Supercomputer Systems: Austria is currently developing a hybrid quantum-HPC solution, which will aid in the integration of practical workflows in the near to medium term.

  • Community Outreach: Events like Quantum Hub Tirol and Quantum Tour 2025 strengthen the ecosystem.
