QURECA

QUantum REsources and CAreers

2025.10.09

QURECA is a company specializing in quantum technology training, recruitment, and business consulting, with a mission to make society "quantum-ready". All its courses and training are CPD certified. It serves a client base that includes corporations, academic institutions, and quantum companies, and provides a quantum resources platform (company map, events, papers).


▲ Qureka! Box unpacks quantum computing concepts through interactive gaming tools, enhancing learning motivation and retention. It provides physical models like the QbitBox and a 3D Bloch Sphere, allowing students to touch, interact with, and assemble them, thereby gaining a solid grasp of fundamental concepts such as spin, uncertainty, superposition, and quantum states.

SpinQ Desktop Quantum Computer


Ref: https://lms.qureca.com/product/spinq-desktop-quantum-computer/?v=255a5cac7685

In collaboration with SPINQ, this is an educational quantum platform targeted at schools and beginners. Based on Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) technology, it provides hands-on operation of a real 2–3 qubit system and a built-in simulator, allowing users to write, execute, and observe quantum circuits in a classroom or self-study setting. The built-in simulator can extend exercises to a larger number of qubits.
Real device: up to ~3 qubits
Simulator: up to ~24 qubits
