Qilimanjaro Quantum Tech

2025.11.17

Qilimanjaro (Headquarters: Barcelona, Spain) is a full-stack analog quantum computing company, focusing on Quantum Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (QASICs) built with superconducting circuits. Through a customized analog quantum roadmap co-designed with clients, it targets high-value simulation, optimization, and AI application scenarios, emphasizing faster, more accurate, and more sustainable solutions, while also paving the way for the development of universal quantum computing.

Products / Services

  • Quantum as a Service (QaaS): Cloud access to its quantum computers and simulators, eliminating upfront setup, focusing on real-time execution and results.

  • Quantum Consulting Services: Analyzing existing workflows, introducing quantum and AI-driven quantum-inspired algorithms to accelerate performance.

  • Quantum Computer Deployment: On-premises deployment of dedicated quantum computers (supporting selection of digital or analog routes), including installation, operation, and maintenance.

  • Quantum ASIC (QASIC) Co-development: Jointly developing analog quantum solutions for specific problems to accelerate the achievement of quantum advantage and enhance return on investment (ROI).
