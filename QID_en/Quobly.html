Quobly

2025.11.06

French startup Quobly aims to drive large-scale quantum computers using mature silicon-based semiconductor processes. By integrating quantum technology into the existing semiconductor ecosystem and ensuring compatibility with current CMOS production lines, they believe it's possible to cross the "million-qubit" threshold, moving quantum computing from the lab to the data center. Quobly is a spin-off from CEA and CNRS, founded in 2022, dedicated to manufacturing silicon-based quantum computers to solve problems that today's most powerful supercomputers cannot.
