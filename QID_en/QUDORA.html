QUDORA

QUDORA Technologies GmbH

2025.11.15

  • Positioning: A German startup focused on building and commercializing "trapped-ion" quantum computers and standalone quantum processing units (QPUs).

  • Mission: To provide customized quantum technology solutions with partners, driving innovation and transformation across industries.

  • Public Offering: Cloud QCaaS (QUDORA Cloud), currently providing a simulator based on realistic trapped-ion noise models, supporting OpenQASM 2/3, Qiskit, and QIR.

Products / Services

  • Hardware

    • Full-stack Quantum Computers: Centered on micro-fabricated ion trap processors, emphasizing highly integrated quantum control.

    • Standalone Quantum Processors (QPUs): Available as independent components or system-embedded modules.

  • Cloud Services (QCaaS)

    • QUDORA Cloud: Provides access to a trapped-ion noise model simulator and algorithm execution (OpenQASM 2/3, Qiskit, QIR).

Technology and R&D

  • Hardware Architecture: Trapped-ion qubits, featuring high operational fidelity, milder operating temperatures, and storage times of up to several minutes, suitable for hybrid computation.

  • Key Innovation: Replacing traditional "bulky, complex" laser control with "microwave electronics." This facilitates high integration and compatibility with semiconductor fabrication processes, paving the way for scalability beyond "thousands of qubits."

  • Process & Integration: Micro-fabricated quantum processors, high on-chip integration of quantum control; compatible with standard semiconductor processes, beneficial for manufacturing scale-up and yield management.
